Almanac

The plot

You fell through a trapdoor into a living glasshouse. Eight floors down, the Heartroot pulses. You will not be rescued. You may still garden.

Hands

  • WASD / arrows — step (bump to swat)
  • 1–5 — choose a seed
  • F — plant in the last step’s direction
  • G — harvest a ripe plant beside you
  • T — water adjacent seedlings
  • R — compost (from slugs) onto a plant you face
  • E — eat the selected seed (poor supper)
  • > / Enter — descend stairs you stand on
  • . — wait a turn · click to step toward a tile

Crops

  • Sunroot — food and blood.
  • Thornvine — a turret. Hurts pests each turn.
  • Glowcap — lights the dark. Harvest a lantern.
  • Coinleaf — money that photosynthesizes.
  • Nightshade — harvest to poison the neighborhood.

Pests

Slugs eat crops and make compost. Aphids rush. Blight moths sicken soil and blood. Topiary knights are slow hedges with opinions. Plant first. Then fight.
